Unemployment and Coronary Disease: A Stress-Induced Trend

By: Caleb Copley

Overview

In my time at UC, one of the most major things I've had to deal with is the difficulty of the CS job market in the space of internships. The sheer stress of that herculean task and coping with the fallout has led me to desire to show/research correlation between heart disease and unemployment in America.

Directions

To view the data, select a state from the dropdown menu. The map will update to show the selected state, and the chart will display the unemployment rate and coronary heart disease rate for that state.

To view the data for all states, select "All States" from the dropdown menu. The map will update to show all states, and the chart will display the average unemployment rate and coronary heart disease rate for all states.
